Enrollment for the coming semester is now open for **new students** and **transferees**.

## What to do first

1. Read the requirements for your category on the [Requirements](/requirements/) page.
2. Prepare your documents. A printable checklist is available on each requirements page.
3. Visit the Registrar's Office during office hours to submit your documents.

## Need help?

Come to the **Enrollment Orientation** on December 12 or contact the Registrar's Office. Our staff are happy to guide you step by step.
